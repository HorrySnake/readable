从 Safari 开始，按自己的习惯读下去。Start in Safari. Read your way.

1. 启用 Safari 扩展1. Enable the Safari extension

iPhone / iPad：在系统设置中找到 Safari → 扩展 → 更好读，启用并允许访问您希望阅读的网站。目前仅 iPhone 和 iPad 版已上架，Mac 版仍在优化中，暂未开放下载。系统版本不同，入口名称可能略有变化。On iPhone or iPad, find Safari → Extensions → Parse in system settings, enable it, and allow your chosen websites. The app is currently available for iPhone and iPad only. The Mac version is still being refined and is not yet available to download. Labels may vary by system version.

2. 阅读与离线章节2. Reading and offline chapters

在支持网页上通过扩展打开阅读模式，调整字体、行距与主题。支持的网页书籍可加入书架；下载完成的章节可离线阅读。如果正文识别失败，请确认网站权限、登录状态与网站结构；解析规则可用于调整支持站点的识别。Open reading mode through the extension on supported pages and adjust fonts, spacing and themes. Add supported web books to your library and read downloaded chapters offline. If extraction fails, check website access, sign-in status and page structure. Parsing rules can adjust recognition for supported sites.
