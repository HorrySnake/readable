1. 适用范围与开发者1. Scope and developer

本政策适用于「更好读 - Reader for Safari」的 iPhone、iPad App、Safari 扩展及本网站。产品由 CoherentStudio 开发与维护。联系邮箱：CoherentStudio@hotmail.com。This policy covers Parse - Browser Reading Extension on iPhone and iPad, its Safari extension, and this website. CoherentStudio develops and maintains the product. Contact: CoherentStudio@hotmail.com.

2. 网页阅读与网站权限2. Web reading and website access

在您允许访问的网站上，扩展会读取网页地址、标题、正文、章节目录及相关书籍信息，用于正文提取、排版、连续阅读和保存。解析在设备上完成；更好读不将您的阅读正文或书架上传至开发者服务器。您可在 Safari 的扩展设置中限制或撤回网站权限；撤回后，相应网站上的阅读功能将无法工作。On websites you allow, the extension reads page URLs, titles, main text, chapter lists and book details to extract, format, continue and save reading content. Parsing takes place on your device. Parse does not upload your reading text or library to a developer server. Restrict or revoke website access in Safari extension settings; reading features on those sites will then be unavailable.

3. 设备上的阅读数据3. Reading data on your device

书架、阅读进度、书签、偏好、解析规则、网站收藏与下载章节保存在设备上的应用存储中。App 与 Safari 扩展通过系统 App Group 共享所需数据。当前版本不提供开发者运营的账号系统或跨设备云同步。系统备份是否包含这些数据，取决于您的 Apple 设备与备份设置。Your library, progress, bookmarks, preferences, parsing rules, website bookmarks and downloaded chapters are stored in app storage on your device. The app and Safari extension share necessary data through the system App Group. The current version has no developer-operated account system or cross-device cloud sync. Inclusion in system backups depends on your Apple device and backup settings.

4. 网站请求、封面与登录会话4. Website requests, covers and sign-in sessions

打开原网页、加载封面、获取目录及下载章节时，设备会向您选择的网站或其资源服务器发起网络请求。这些网站可能获得 IP 地址、请求地址及常规网络信息。App 内网页视图会保存网站的 Cookie 和登录会话；为获取您已获授权的页面，下载请求可能复用与目标网站匹配的会话 Cookie。更好读不要求您向开发者提供网站密码。第三方网站自行处理其数据，并适用其隐私政策。Opening source pages, loading covers, fetching chapter lists and downloading chapters sends requests to your chosen sites or their resource servers. Those sites may receive your IP address, requested URLs and ordinary network information. In-app web views retain site cookies and sign-in sessions; download requests may reuse session cookies matching the destination site to retrieve pages you are authorized to access. Parse does not ask you to provide website passwords to the developer. Third-party sites handle their own data under their own policies.

5. 购买与诊断5. Purchases and diagnostics

App 内购买通过 Apple StoreKit 处理。更好读在设备上读取购买结果和权益状态，以提供购买、恢复及相关功能；开发者不接收您的银行卡资料。Apple 可能按其规则向开发者提供销售报表，以及在您允许时提供诊断信息。当前代码未集成第三方广告、行为统计或自动上传阅读数据的 SDK。内部运行日志用于排错；请勿在反馈中发送密码、Cookie 或无关的私人正文。In-app purchases are handled by Apple StoreKit. The app reads purchase results and entitlement status on your device for purchases, restoration and related features; the developer does not receive payment card details. Apple may provide sales reports and, when you allow it, diagnostics under its rules. The current code includes no third-party advertising, behavioral analytics or SDK that automatically uploads reading data. Internal runtime logs support troubleshooting. Do not include passwords, cookies or unrelated private text in feedback.

6. 保留、删除与您的选择6. Retention, deletion and your choices

本地数据会保留到您在 App 中删除、清理或卸载应用；具体数据类别的删除入口包括书架管理、书签管理与离线内容清理。Safari 扩展存储、网站 Cookie 和系统备份可能独立保留，请在 Safari、相关网页视图的可用设置或系统备份设置中管理；卸载 App 不保证清除所有这些副本。您可以向联系邮箱提出隐私问题或关于您主动提供的反馈信息的访问、更正与删除请求。开发者无法远程读取或删除您的本地书架。Local data remains until you remove it in the app, clear it, or uninstall the app. Available controls include library management, bookmark management and offline-content cleanup. Safari extension storage, site cookies and system backups may remain separately; manage these in Safari, available web-view controls or system backup settings. Uninstalling the app does not guarantee removal of all such copies. Email us with privacy questions or requests to access, correct or delete information you voluntarily sent in feedback. The developer cannot remotely read or delete your local library.

Share only necessary information. You may request deletion of feedback after your issue is resolved, except records that must be retained by law.
