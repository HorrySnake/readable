1.1 本政策适用于 CoherentStudio（以下简称“我们”）提供的更好读（英文名 Parse）iPhone、iPad App、Safari 扩展及官网。本政策说明本产品如何访问、使用、存储与提供信息；您访问的网站、云盘、Apple 等第三方服务另有各自的隐私规则。功能以您实际安装的已发布版本为准。1.1 This policy covers the Parse iPhone and iPad app, its Safari extension and official website, provided by CoherentStudio (“we”). It explains how the product accesses, uses, stores and discloses information. Websites, cloud providers, Apple and other independent services have their own privacy practices. Features depend on the released version you use.

1.2 使用基本阅读功能无需注册开发者账号。我们不运营集中托管您书库或阅读正文的账号数据库，不接入广告、跨 App 跟踪或第三方行为分析服务。您主动连接第三方服务、发起购买或发送反馈时，仍会发生下文说明的信息处理，不能将“本地优先”理解为所有功能都不联网。1.2 Basic reading does not require a developer-operated account. We do not operate a central account database hosting your library or reading text, or integrate advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party behavioral analytics. Connecting a provider, purchasing or sending feedback still involves the processing described below; local-first operation does not mean that every feature is offline.

2. 阅读数据与本机处理2. Reading data and on-device processing

2.1 为提供阅读、书架管理、连续阅读和听书，App 在设备上处理书籍或文章的名称、来源地址、作者、封面、目录、正文、阅读进度、书签、阅读笔记、网站收藏、解析规则、下载记录与阅读偏好。您导入本机、系统文件选择器或云盘中的文件时，还会处理所选文件的名称、类型、内容和必要的目录信息。2.1 To provide reading, library management, continuous reading and listening, the app processes titles, source URLs, authors, covers, chapter lists and text, reading progress, bookmarks, notes, website bookmarks, parsing rules, download records and preferences on your device. Importing from local storage, the system file picker or a cloud drive also involves selected file names, types, content and necessary directory information.

2.2 App 与 Safari 扩展通过系统共享容器交换书架、进度、设置及相关功能所需的数据。这是同一设备上的协作，不是开发者提供的跨设备云同步。通过“文件”导入 iCloud 中的文件，也不代表 App 会将整个书库自动同步到 iCloud。听书使用系统语音合成能力；语音资源的下载和系统服务由 Apple 管理。2.2 The app and Safari extension exchange library data, progress, settings and other necessary information through the system shared container on the same device. This is not developer-operated cross-device cloud synchronization. Importing an iCloud file through Files does not automatically synchronize your library to iCloud. Listening uses system speech synthesis; Apple manages voice-resource downloads and system services.

3. Safari 权限与网页会话3. Safari access and webpage sessions

3.1 在您启用扩展并允许访问的网站上，扩展可读取页面地址、标题、正文、目录、相关链接与页面结构，以完成提取、排版、章节导航和保存。App 内浏览器也会按您打开的页面处理这些信息。我们不将您的浏览历史或阅读正文集中上传给开发者。您保存的阅读条目本身可能包含原始网页地址。3.1 On sites you authorize after enabling the extension, it can read page URLs, titles, text, chapter lists, related links and page structure for extraction, layout, navigation and saving. The in-app browser processes the pages you open in a similar way. We do not centrally upload browsing history or reading text to the developer. Saved reading items may themselves contain source URLs.

3.2 网站可在浏览器中保存 Cookie 和登录会话。为了获取您有权访问的内容，相关请求可能使用与目标网站匹配的会话信息；这些信息不作为反馈自动发给我们。您可在 Safari 的扩展与网站权限设置中限制或撤回访问。撤回会影响该网站上的功能，但不会自动删除已经保存的正文、书架或网站自身保留的数据。3.2 Sites can store cookies and sign-in sessions in browser storage. Requests may use session information matching the destination site to access content you are entitled to read; this information is not automatically sent to us as feedback. Restrict or revoke access in Safari extension and website settings. Revocation affects features on that site but does not automatically delete saved text, library items or data retained by the site.

4. 网络请求与第三方网站4. Network requests and third-party sites

4.1 打开网页、搜索或跳转到外部服务、获取目录、加载封面、检查规则与下载内容时，设备会连接您选择的网站及其必要资源服务器。接收方可能获得请求地址、搜索内容、IP 地址、常规网络标识以及请求所需的认证信息，并按其规则记录访问。第三方页面可能自行加载广告、统计或其他资源，这些不是本网站的统计脚本。4.1 Opening pages, searching or following external services, retrieving chapter lists, loading covers, checking rules and downloading content connects your device to selected sites and necessary resource servers. Recipients may receive requested URLs, search queries, IP addresses, ordinary network identifiers and required authentication information, and log access under their own rules. Third-party pages may load their own advertising, analytics or other resources.

4.2 我们仅为完成您请求的功能使用相关信息。请核实网站和服务地址，并优先使用 HTTPS；您自行配置的 HTTP 服务可能无法保护传输内容。第三方服务可能在其他国家或地区处理数据，其保留、删除与安全措施由该服务决定；我们不承诺第三方“零保留”。依法需要告知、单独同意或履行跨境程序的处理，应在相关处理前完成。4.2 We use relevant information to perform the features you request. Verify destinations and prefer HTTPS; a configured HTTP service may not protect data in transit. Providers may process information in other countries and apply their own retention, deletion and security practices. We do not guarantee third-party zero retention. Required notices, separate consent and international-transfer procedures must be completed before relevant processing.

5. 云盘、书库与局域网连接5. Cloud drives, libraries and local networks

5.1 您主动配置 WebDAV、OPDS、SMB/CIFS 或已提供的云盘连接后，App 会为验证连接、列出目录和下载或导入所选文件，处理服务器地址、账户标识、目录、文件元数据和文件内容。连接局域网书库或 NAS 时，系统可能请求本地网络权限。拒绝该权限或不连接云盘，不影响不依赖这些服务的阅读功能。5.1 When you configure WebDAV, OPDS, SMB/CIFS or an available cloud-drive connection, the app processes server addresses, account identifiers, directories, file metadata and content to verify access, list folders and download or import chosen files. Connecting to a local library or NAS may require local-network permission. Declining that permission or cloud access does not prevent reading that does not depend on those services.

5.2 您填写的连接凭据，以及 OAuth 访问令牌和刷新令牌，使用设备 Keychain 保存，以支持后续认证。采用第三方登录时，账户密码由服务商的授权页面处理，App 不要求将该密码交给开发者。请求通常从设备直接发往所选服务；若某项配置使用您指定的令牌交换后端，该后端也会接收完成交换所需的授权码或令牌等信息，请核实其运营者和隐私规则。5.2 Connection credentials and OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in the device Keychain for authentication. Provider sign-in handles passwords through its authorization page; the app does not ask you to give the developer that password. Requests normally go directly from your device to the selected provider. If a configuration uses a token-exchange backend you specify, that backend also receives authorization codes or tokens needed for the exchange. Verify its operator and privacy practices.

6. Google Drive 的专项说明6. Google Drive disclosures

6.1 连接 Google Drive 是可选功能。Google 登录组件可能处理经授权的账户标识、姓名、邮箱、头像等基本账户资料，以完成登录和识别账户。Parse 申请 Drive 只读访问（drive.readonly）；该权限的技术范围允许读取授权账户的 Drive 文件，并非只对一个选中文件生效。实际功能用于您浏览目录时获取文件名称、标识、类型、大小等元数据，以及下载或导入您选择的文件，不申请通过 Drive API 新建、修改或删除文件的权限。6.1 Google Drive is optional. Google Sign-In may process authorized identifiers, names, email addresses, photos and basic account information to authenticate and identify an account. Parse requests read-only Drive access (drive.readonly). This scope technically permits reading files in the authorized account, not just a single selected file. The feature retrieves metadata such as names, identifiers, types and sizes when you browse folders, and downloads or imports files you choose. It does not request Drive API permissions to create, modify or delete files.

6.2 Google API 数据仅用于上述用户可见的连接、文件浏览、导入和阅读功能。我们不出售这些数据，不将其用于广告、用户画像或训练通用 AI 模型，也不将其发送给广告平台或数据经纪人。我们不会在后台人工阅读您的 Drive 文件；只有您主动提供材料以获得支持，或适用规则允许的安全与法律处理等必要情况，才在相应范围内处理收到的材料。6.2 Google API data is used only for the user-facing connection, file browsing, import and reading features described above. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, profiling or training general-purpose AI models, or send it to advertising platforms or data brokers. We do not routinely have humans read your Drive files. Materials you voluntarily provide for support, and necessary security or legal processing permitted by applicable rules, are handled only within the relevant scope.

6.3 Parse 对通过 Google API 获得的信息的使用和向其他应用转移，遵守 Google API Services User Data Policy，包括其中的 Limited Use 要求。您可在 App 中移除云端连接，并在 Google 账户的第三方连接设置中撤销授权。移除连接或撤销授权不会自动删除已经导入本机的文件；本机副本需另行删除。6.3 Parse’s use and transfer to other apps of information received from Google APIs adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Remove the connection in the app and revoke access in your Google Account third-party connections. Disconnecting or revoking access does not automatically erase files already imported to your device; delete local copies separately.

7. 自定义规则与 AI 辅助解析7. Custom rules and AI-assisted parsing

7.1 当前 AI 辅助解析流程在本机生成提示词，供您自行复制或导出给所选 AI，再导入返回的规则。材料可能包含网站地址、标题、正文样本和您主动采集的精简 HTML。App 不会因为您进入此功能而自动将这些材料上传给 AI 服务。复制、分享或导出前，请检查是否含有账户信息、个人资料、认证内容或未经授权的作品。7.1 The current AI-assisted workflow builds prompts locally for you to copy or export to an AI service you choose, then import its returned rules. Materials may contain site URLs, titles, text samples and simplified HTML you deliberately capture. Opening this feature does not automatically upload those materials to an AI provider. Before copying, sharing or exporting, check for account information, personal data, authentication material or works you are not authorized to share.

7.2 您向外部 AI、其他应用或分享目标提供的内容，随后由相应接收方按其规则处理。我们不能撤回已发送的内容，也不能保证其不用于训练或不被保留。导入的规则可能指定网络请求地址，请核实来源和内容；不要导入不可信规则。7.2 When you give material to an external AI, another app or a sharing destination, that recipient subsequently handles it under its own rules. We cannot recall submitted content or guarantee exclusion from training or retention. Imported rules may specify network destinations. Check their origin and content, and avoid untrusted rules.

Diagnostic exports may also include app/build and system versions and shared-container status. These logs are not automatically uploaded to us. Use available log cleanup and export controls. 10.1 阅读资料、导入文件及配置主要保存在设备的应用存储、共享容器或 Safari 扩展存储中，部分数据有本地恢复副本。您可管理书架、书签、笔记与规则，并通过存储管理清理缓存、下载或本机数据。具体清理范围以操作提示为准；清理普通阅读数据不应被视为已删除独立保存的所有云盘凭据、Safari Cookie 或系统备份。10.1 Reading material, imported files and settings are mainly stored in app storage, the shared container or Safari extension storage, with local recovery copies for some data. Manage library items, bookmarks, notes and rules, and use storage controls for caches, downloads or device data. Check each action’s stated scope. Uninstalling is not the same as revoking provider access, canceling subscriptions or deleting copies elsewhere. Keychain items, Safari data, provider servers and device backups may retain information independently. We do not hold your full local library and cannot remotely erase your device or data already received by third parties.
